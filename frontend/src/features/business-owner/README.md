# Business owner workspace

The `/business-owner` portal uses the `BUSINESS_OWNER` role and supports multiple businesses per account.

- `api` - business owner endpoint functions
- `context` - selected business and business switching
- `components` - owner navigation and selected-business guard
- `pages` - overview, profile, services, staff, hours, holidays, appointments, and pricing rules
- `owner.css` - responsive owner workspace styles

The registration page already supports business owner signup. Owners create a business profile from the portal before opening its management pages.
